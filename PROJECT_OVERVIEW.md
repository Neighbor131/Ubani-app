## Concept
Ubani is a hyperlocal community app — a place for people to connect, share, and stay informed within their immediate neighborhood (block, street, or building).

## Stack
- Next.js (App Router)
- Tailwind + shadcn/ui
- Supabase (DB, Auth, Realtime)
- Prisma ORM
- Mapbox (location & radius)
- PWA-ready

## Brand
- Primary color: #98CE00  
- Typeface: Pally (headlines), General Sans (body)

## Core Features
- Location-based onboarding  
- Feed with errands, alerts, marketplace posts  
- 1:1 chat tied to posts  
- Community badges & local stats  
- Push notifications (Web/PWA)
